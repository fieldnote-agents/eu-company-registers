EEEstonia

e-Business Register (e-Äriregister)

Centre of Registers and Information Systems (RIK). API access, keys, rate limits and terms, each with its official source. Last checked 17 Oct 2026.

Official sitehttps://ariregister.rik.ee/eng
Source: https://www.rik.ee/en/e-business-register/business-register-queries · checked 17 Oct 2026
Free APIYes, after signing a contract with RIK; an API-only contract is free of charge. Daily open-data files are also free to download.
Source: https://avaandmed.ariregister.rik.ee/en/open-data-api/introduction-api-services · checked 17 Oct 2026
API docshttps://avaandmed.ariregister.rik.ee/en/open-data-api/introduction-api-services
Source: https://avaandmed.ariregister.rik.ee/en/open-data-api/introduction-api-services · checked 17 Oct 2026
Key / accountYes. A signed contract; REST services use an API key (/keyLogin/), XML services a username and password. Autocomplete and e-invoice-recipient queries need no contract.
Source: https://avaandmed.ariregister.rik.ee/en/business-registry-rest-api/api-key-authentication · checked 17 Oct 2026
Data availableEight open-data datasets (JSON/XML, some CSV), updated daily; API: general and detailed company data, annual reports, beneficial owners, representation rights and more. Since 1 Nov 2024 open-data files exclude personal ID codes.
Source: https://avaandmed.ariregister.rik.ee/en/downloading-open-data · checked 17 Oct 2026
Rate limits50,000 answers/day and 1 simultaneous query per contract partner; 20 requests/minute per IP for document downloads.
Source: https://avaandmed.ariregister.rik.ee/en/open-data-api/introduction-api-services · checked 17 Oct 2026
Licence / termsDownloading requires accepting the licence terms; the site's licence link points to CC BY 4.0.
Source: https://avaandmed.ariregister.rik.ee/en/downloading-open-data · checked 17 Oct 2026
